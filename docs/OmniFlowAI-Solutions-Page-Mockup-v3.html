What changed, and why

  • Hero system visual. Disconnected hexagon nodes on the left, orange connectors drawing into a single growth system on the right. It states the thesis before a word is read — and it's line art, not illustration, so it stays inside the engineering positioning.
  • Trust strip immediately after the hero, answering "who are these people" at the moment the question forms. Compact, mono, no logos — the logo wall stays on the homepage.
  • Problem section rebuilt. The five pains are now large display type on hairline rules, not small bullets. Below them, a before → after chain makes the transition visible instead of implied.
  • Router numbered 01–06 with display-weight statements. It reads as a diagnostic instrument rather than a form, and the selected state is the only orange fill in the section.
  • Cards cut to roughly half their previous first-view text. Each opens with the problem in the client's words, then one outcome line, then price. Best for, the problem detail, and the inclusion list move into the accordion.
  • Custom Transformation is now a full orange inversion — the single highest-contrast moment on the page, and the only section that breaks the dark surface. It reads as a deliberate break, which is exactly what an escape hatch should feel like.
  • Section rhythm alternates surfaces — ink-950, ink-900, ink-950, ink-900, orange, ink-950, ink-900 — so scrolling has cadence instead of one continuous dark field.
  • Restraint held. No new colours beyond the existing palette, no illustration, no stock photography, no gradients except two radial accent glows, and one small hero animation that runs once.
