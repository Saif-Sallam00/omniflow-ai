Homepage render order: **Hero → Trust bar → Value Prop → Pillars → Transform (before/after) → Proof + Recent Work → How We Work → Mid-page CTA band → Final CTA.** Primary CTA throughout is `common.cta.bookCall` (`احجز مكالمة استراتيجية`); hero secondary is `استعرض أعمالنا`.

**Status:** Audit only — no edits made to `i18n.tsx`. Awaiting approval.

## 1. Hero
`نبني الأنظمة التي تقف خلف نمو الأعمال.` / sub: `شريكك في التحول الرقمي عبر تدريب الذكاء الاصطناعي والتسويق الرقمي وبرمجيات الأعمال — مصمَّمة كنظام واحد متكامل، لا كأربع خدمات منفصلة.` / CTAs: `احجز مكالمة استراتيجية` · `استعرض أعمالنا`

1. **Headline** — Good, evocative, on-brand (you chose to keep it). Slightly *we-focused* ("we build") but works. ✓
2. **Subheadline value prop** — Clear-ish but **feature-led** (lists services) and opens with a category label (`شريكك في التحول الرقمي`) rather than the buyer's outcome.
3. **Customer vs. us** — Leans *us* ("we build," "your partner," our service list).
4. **Concrete benefits** — Weak: names *what we do*, not *what the owner gets* (fewer tools, one system, measurable growth).
5. **Weak/forgettable** — ⚠️ **Factual defect:** the sub lists **three** services then contrasts with `لا كأربع خدمات منفصلة` ("not **four**"). Three-vs-four mismatch undermines the one-integrated-system claim exactly where it's made.
6. **Leads to next** — Yes, flows into the trust bar then the "systems problem" value prop. ✓
7. **Objections** — None answered yet (fine for a hero).
8. **Trust** — Neutral; relies on the logo bar right below.
9. **CTA** — `احجز مكالمة استراتيجية` is a strong B2B primary. ✓ Secondary is fine.
10. **Score: 7/10** — strong bones, undercut by the 4-vs-3 error and feature-led sub.

**Change (high impact):**
- **Current:** `شريكك في التحول الرقمي عبر تدريب الذكاء الاصطناعي والتسويق الرقمي وبرمجيات الأعمال — مصمَّمة كنظام واحد متكامل، لا كأربع خدمات منفصلة.`
- **Problem:** Numeric inconsistency (3 listed, "four" contrasted); opens with a self-label + service list instead of the buyer's payoff.
- **Improved:** `نُوحّد تدريب الذكاء الاصطناعي والتسويق الرقمي وبرمجيات الأعمال في نظام واحد متكامل تملكه وتقيسه — بدل ثلاث خدمات منفصلة لا يربطها شيء.`
- **Why it converts:** Fixes the credibility-killing number error; leads with the outcome (one system you *own and measure*) and sharpens the pain (`لا يربطها شيء`) that makes integration valuable to an operator.

## 6. Proof + Recent Work
`نُقاس بالنتائج، لا بالمخرجات` / `كل مشروع مرتبط بأثر تلمسه أعمالك — إيرادات، كفاءة، تكلفة استقطاب، توسّع. وهذه هي الأعمال التي تقف وراء ذلك.` → project grid.

1. **Headline — excellent** ("measured by results, not deliverables"). ✓ 2. Value prop — clear. ✓ 3. Customer-focused — yes. ✓ 4. **Concrete — NO. This is the biggest conversion gap on the page.** The section *promises* results (revenue, efficiency, CAC, scale) but shows **zero numbers** — just project cards. A "results" section with no metric is the classic trust leak. 5. Weak — the body writes a check the section doesn't cash. 6. Leads to next — ✓. 7. Objections — "prove it" is raised and *left unanswered*. 8. **Trust — undercut**: claiming result-focus without a single figure can read as weaker than saying nothing. 9. CTA — `عرض جميع الأعمال` ✓. 10. **Score: 5/10 — highest upside on the page.**

**Change (highest impact — content, needs real data):**
- **Current:** body claims impact; grid shows only images/titles.
- **Problem:** Results claim with no evidence. SME/GCC buyers convert on concrete outcomes.
- **Improved:** Add a **3-stat proof strip** under the headline, e.g. `+٣٠٪ نمو في الإيرادات` · `‎-٤٠٪ في العمل اليدوي` · `‎٤× أسرع في التسليم` — **each pulled from a real case (the frozen `TODO(Layer3-proof)` slots), never invented.** Alternatively surface one metric per project card.
- **Why it converts:** Quantified outcomes are the single strongest B2B conversion driver; they turn a portfolio into evidence. This likely outperforms every wording tweak combined. *Requires your real numbers before shipping.*

## The 10 Highest-Impact Copy Improvements

*Ranked by expected conversion impact.*

1. **Add a real 3-stat proof strip to the Proof section** (§6) — quantified outcomes from the frozen case-study slots. *Biggest lever; needs your real numbers.*
2. **Fix the hero sub's 3-vs-4 error + make it outcome-led** (§1) — removes a credibility crack at the top of the funnel.
3. **Put a real client count in the trust bar** (§2) — `+20 شركة…` beats an abstract adjective. *Needs the real count.*
4. **Add a risk-reversal subheadline to "How We Work"** (§7) — kills scope-creep/lock-in objections mid-page.
5. **Reframe the "How We Work" headline** to `كيف نعمل — بلا مفاجآت` (§7) — turns a generic block into a promise.
6. **Surface one metric per project card** in Recent Work (§6) — if a full stat strip isn't ready, even one number per card lifts trust. *Real data.*
7. **Add an ownership micro-line near the pillars/hero** ("تملك كل ما نبنيه") — ownership is a top GCC/SME objection, currently buried on inner pages.
8. **Tighten the value-prop body by one clause** (§3) — it's strong but slightly long; trimming raises scan-through. *Low risk, low-moderate lift.*
9. **Lock the positioning term** `شريك التحول الرقمي` everywhere (§4) — consistency compounds recall/trust. *Cosmetic but cheap.*
10. **Add a specificity cue to the hero secondary CTA** (e.g. `استعرض أعمالنا` → `استعرض دراسات الحالة`) — sets a higher-intent expectation for the portfolio click. *Smallest lift.*
